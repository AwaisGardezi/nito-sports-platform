NITO SPORTS designs, cuts, prints, embroiders and packs custom sportswear in Sialkot — the city that has supplied the world's sports goods and leather exports for more than a century. We are the factory, not a trading house between you and one.

Who we are

Built around making the thing, not reselling it.

Sialkot has been manufacturing for export since the 19th century, and the skill base here — pattern cutters, stitchers, embroidery operators, sublimation printers — is the reason global brands have sourced from this city for generations. NITO SPORTS is part of that tradition, working directly with clubs, gyms, sports brands and private-label clothing brands instead of selling through retail channels.

That model changes how we work. There is no season, no stock and no shelf. Every order starts with your artwork and ends with your branded parcel leaving our facility. It means we can offer genuinely low minimums, direct factory pricing, and unlimited colourways — because nothing is printed until you have approved it.

Capability

What happens on our floor.

Pattern making & grading

Custom blocks built from your measurements or from a garment you send us, graded across youth and adult size runs.

Cutting & stitching

Fabric inspection, laying, cutting and assembly. Flatlock, overlock, coverstitch and bar-tack reinforcement where the garment needs it.

Sublimation printing

Full-surface dye sublimation — unlimited colours, photographic detail, no per-colour charge, and graphics that will not crack or peel off the fabric.

Embroidery

Multi-head embroidery for crests, monograms and 3D puff. Also appliqué, tackle twill and chenille patches for varsity and heritage work.

Labels & packaging

Woven main labels, printed care and size labels, hangtags, polybags and carton branding — produced under your brand, not ours.

Quality control

In-line checks during stitching plus a final inspection covering measurement, stitching, print registration and labelling before anything is packed.
