PAYMENT MARKS — PROVENANCE
==========================
Retrieved: 2026-10-09

Every file here is the provider's OWN published logo artwork, downloaded
unmodified. Nothing was redrawn, re-coloured, re-typeset or lifted from a
screenshot. Same rule as assets/img/scci.png and assets/img/fbr.png.

These are third-party trademarks. They are displayed only to indicate which
transfer methods NITO SPORTS accepts ("nominative use"), and index.html carries
a visible attribution line saying so. NITO SPORTS is not an agent of any of
these providers — a buyer sends the transfer through the provider of their
choice.

If the owner would rather not carry third-party marks at all, delete the `logo`
field for that entry in assets/js/catalog.js; the card falls back to showing the
method name as text, which is exactly what a method with no brand mark does.

LICENSING NOTE FOR THE OWNER
----------------------------
Logos are protected as trademarks independently of copyright, so a Commons
("free") file is still a trademark. Using a provider's mark to say "we accept
this method" is standard practice on exporter and e-commerce sites, and the
attribution line in index.html keeps the relationship unambiguous. If any
provider objects, remove that entry's `logo` field — the section keeps working.
