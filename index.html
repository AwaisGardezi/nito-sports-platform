Why buyers work with us

A factory that answers the questions you actually ask.

Most sourcing problems are not about price — they are about consistency, communication and whether the second order looks like the first one. These are the things we control directly.

Ordering from Pakistan for the first time?

Start with a sample. We produce a pre-production piece in your exact fabric and colourway so you can check the hand feel, the fit and the print quality before you commit to bulk. Sample cost is credited back on qualifying bulk orders.

Cut and sewn in-house

Pattern making, grading, cutting and stitching all happen on our own floor. Nothing is subcontracted to a third party you have never spoken to.

Printing and embroidery under one roof

Sublimation, screen print, DTF, tackle twill and multi-head embroidery. Your crest, sponsor marks and player names are produced in the same building that cuts the garment.

Pantone-matched colour

Send a Pantone reference or a physical swatch. We match to it and keep the recipe on file, so your repeat order in eight months comes out the same colour.

Private label, fully built

Woven main labels, care labels, size labels, hangtags, polybags and carton branding. The parcel that reaches your customer looks like your brand — not ours.

Export paperwork handled

Commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin prepared for you. We ship air express, air freight or sea freight depending on your volume and deadline.

One person on your order

You deal with the same contact from first quote to final shipment. No handovers, no re-explaining your artwork to someone new every week.

How ordering works

From your artwork to your door, in four steps.

01

Send your requirement

Fill in the enquiry form with your product, quantity, sizes, delivery country and target date. Attach any artwork, logo files or reference photos you already have — a rough phone photo of a garment is enough to start.

02

Quote and free mock-up

We come back with factory pricing against your quantity, fabric options and lead time — plus a digital mock-up of the product in your colours and branding. No charge, no obligation.

03

Sample, then production

Approve the mock-up, then we produce a pre-production sample for your sign-off. Once approved, bulk production starts — 10 to 20 days depending on the range.

04

Packed and shipped

Goods are checked, labelled, bagged and cartoned. We send you photographs before dispatch, then ship by air express, air freight or sea freight with full export documents.

Export markets

We ship worldwide.

Our buyers are clubs, academies, gyms, sports brands and private-label clothing brands — not retail shoppers. Orders are shipped internationally by air express, air freight or sea freight, quoted to your door or your nearest port.

  • Air freight — best balance of cost and speed for mid-volume
