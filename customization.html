Decoration methods

Six ways we apply your branding.

The right method depends on the fabric, the artwork and how the garment will be washed. We will recommend one when we quote — and we do all of them in-house.

Sublimation printing

Best for: football, cricket, basketball, hockey, cycling, esports and any all-over design. Unlimited colours, no per-colour cost, graphics fused into the fabric so they cannot crack, peel or fade out of the wash.

No colour limitAll-over print

Screen printing

Best for: bold logos on cotton and fleece — tees, hoodies, sweatshirts, gym wear. Cost-efficient at volume with solid, opaque colour. Puff, metallic and high-density finishes available.

Puff & high-densityBest value at volume

Embroidery

Best for: club crests, polos, caps, hoodies, tracksuits and anything that needs to look premium and last. Flat, 3D puff and chenille. Multi-head machines, so we can run your full order in one pass.

Premium finish3D puff

Appliqué & tackle twill

Best for: varsity jackets, baseball, ice hockey and heritage jerseys. Fabric letters and numbers stitched onto the garment — the traditional, heavyweight look that printed graphics cannot copy.

Heritage lookStitched, not printed

Heat transfer & DTF

Best for: small runs, player names and numbers, and detailed multi-colour artwork without a screen setup. Excellent detail on stretch fabrics where a screen print would crack.

No setup costGreat detail

Logos and designs you send us are used only to produce your order. We do not resell your artwork, add you to a client list, or reuse your design for another buyer.

From artwork to shipment

How a custom order runs.

01

You send the brief

Product, quantity, sizes, colourway, delivery country and target date — plus your logo and any reference images. A rough brief is fine.

02

We quote and mock up

Factory pricing against your quantity, plus a free digital mock-up of the product in your colours with your branding applied.

03

Sample and approval

We produce a pre-production sample in your fabric and colourway. You check fit, hand feel, stitching and print. Adjustments are made until it is right.

04

Bulk, QC, dispatch

Bulk production, in-line and final quality inspection, photographs sent to you for approval, then packing and export shipment.
